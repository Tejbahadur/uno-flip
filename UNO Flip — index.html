UNO Flip

Light meets dark. Play the Flip card to turn the whole table around.

How to play

Goal: Be the first to empty your hand.

Play: Match the top discard by colour or by symbol/number. Wilds may be played anytime.

Light side (white border): Draw One, Skip, Reverse, Flip, Wild, Wild Draw Two.

Dark side (black border): Draw Five, Skip Everyone, Reverse, Flip, Wild, Wild Draw Color.

Flip: A Flip card turns every card over — the other side is now in play, including a new active colour.

Wild Draw Color: Name a colour; the next player keeps drawing until that colour appears, then loses their turn.

UNO! When you reach one card, press the UNO button before ending your turn, or draw a 2-card penalty.
